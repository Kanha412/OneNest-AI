OneNest AI respects your privacy. This policy explains what information is collected, how it is used, and how you can control your data.

Information we collect

  • Account details such as name and email address.
  • Content you create in Notes, Tasks, Expenses, Documents, and Health modules.
  • Technical diagnostics needed to keep the app secure and stable.

How we use your data

  • To deliver app features and personalize your experience.
  • To improve reliability, performance, and security.
  • To respond to support requests and prevent abuse.

Storage and retention

Your data is stored in application databases and file storage linked to your account. If you delete your account, associated data is permanently removed according to system behavior.

Your choices

  • You can update profile and preference settings from the Settings page.
  • You can delete uploaded files and clear storage from Storage settings.
  • You can permanently delete your account from Security settings.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact: privacy@onenest.ai
